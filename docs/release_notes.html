1Cost of Fund — your confirmed rule, live 10 JUL

The Cost of Fund calculation now follows the rule confirmed with you, verified against all 534 rows of your own analysis sheet:

  • Zero within grace. Nothing is charged while a shipment stays within the 10-day grace window after its Estimated ETD.
  • Retroactive once grace is exceeded. The moment the grace period is missed, the charge counts from the Estimated ETD itself (the full delay, not just the days past grace).
  • Early shipment = notional gain. Shipping before the Estimated ETD shows a negative amount, exactly as in your sheet.
  • 12% per annum, matching your sheet (previously 18%). Both the rate and the grace days remain admin-configurable.
  • Unshipped orders accrue day by day once past grace, so exposure stays visible and current.

One deliberate difference from your sheet: shipments arriving within grace show 0 in the system, whereas the sheet charges them a small amount. This follows the rule you confirmed on 10 July. All historical records were recalculated under the new rule.

Recording the Ship Date — stopping the charge 10 JUL

Payment is processed (and the record locked) long before goods actually ship — previously that meant nobody could enter the real ship date, so the Cost of Fund kept accruing forever. Now:

  • The Ship Date has its own dedicated box on the payment page that stays open even after the record is locked.
  • Super Admin, Finance Admin and Accounts Team can enter — or correct — it at any time. Every entry is written to the audit trail with the old and new value and who made it.
  • The moment the date is saved, the Cost of Fund freezes at that date (or becomes a notional gain if the date beat the Estimated ETD).
  • Finance Admins can now open the payment detail page (read-only apart from the Ship Date), reaching it through the Analytics screens.

Shown in the transaction's own currency 10 JUL

A EUR request's Cost of Fund now displays as €…, a CNY request's as ¥… — matching its deposit, everywhere a per-request figure appears. (The amount was always computed correctly in the request's currency; only the symbol was previously shown as $.) Company-wide “Total Cost of Fund” tiles still sum all currencies together and are indicative — the per-currency views remain the authoritative monetary breakdowns.

2Finding requests — search, sort & the invoice number 10–11 JUL

Search on every request list

A search box now sits above the tables on the Merchandiser “My Requests” page, the Accounts payment queue (serving the Pending / On Hold / All tabs), the HoM approval queue, the Admin Overview table, the User Management page, and the analytics drill-down views. One box matches:

  • Request # (ADT-…)
  • Sunshine Invoice #
  • Supplier Invoice #
  • Supplier name
  • Customer name

Results narrow as you type; the × clears back to the full list.

Sort options

Next to each search box: Newest first · Oldest first · Largest amount first · Smallest amount first. Sorting is correct across pages on the large lists. The accounts Pending queue keeps its “oldest first — process in order” discipline until someone actively chooses a different order.

Sunshine Invoice # as the visible number

All request list tables now show the Sunshine Invoice # as the identifying number once Accounts enters it. Until then the internal request number (ADT-YYYY-NNNNN) stands in, so no row is ever blank. The ADT number remains the permanent reference on detail pages, audit logs, notifications and report exports — and the search box finds a request by either number.

Invoice number corrections — Super Admin only, fully tracked 11 JUL

Because the invoice number is now the visible identifier, changing it after a request exists is a controlled action:

  • Only a Super Admin can update the Sunshine Invoice # or Supplier Invoice # on an existing request — every other role is refused by the system itself, not just the screen. Entering the numbers when a request is first created is unchanged.
  • A dedicated “Invoice Numbers” panel on the request's payment page (visible to Super Admins only) is where corrections are made — it works even on locked, processed records.
  • Every change is written to the audit log showing the old value, the new value, who made the change and when — visible under Admin → Audit Logs and included in the audit CSV export.

4Notifications & mobile 10 JUL

  • Payment notifications with the TT copy. When Accounts processes a payment and uploads the bank's TT copy, the requesting merchandiser is notified instantly — in-app bell and a push notification on their phone — with the document link attached. A fallback notice goes out if the TT copy hasn't been uploaded within an hour of processing.
  • Full notifications page. The bell's quick view now opens into a dedicated page listing every notification with paging, unread indicators, TT-copy links and mark-all-read.
  • Push opt-in. After signing in, every user is invited once to enable push notifications; a small reminder appears later for those who skipped it, and it can always be turned on from Settings.
  • Mobile app polish. On phones with the app installed, the page header no longer sits under the phone's clock/status bar, and the notification panel no longer overflows the screen edge.

5Data privacy — analytics scoped by role 11 JUL

A review found that two analytics views — the month-on-month trend charts and the shipment KPIs — showed company-wide figures to merchandisers. This is fixed: merchandisers now see only their own requests in every analytics number, chart and trend, consistent with the rest of the system. Admin, Finance and Accounts views are unchanged (still company-wide). Access to the specialised analytics tabs remains controlled per role from Admin → Analytics Access.
